CI Basics (Expanded)

Manual cross-reference

Use these buttons to open the Field Guide exactly where the topic is explained.

1) What CI is (simple + practical)
  • CI is a public health process to stop infections from spreading.
  • You interview a person with a priority infection (the “index case”), identify contacts, and support confidential notification.
  • You document properly (IR + FR) and follow up outcomes for reporting.
2) Confidentiality (what it means in CI work)
  • Keep personal details private.
  • Do not reveal diagnosis to contacts.
  • Use codes when documenting and following up.
  • Protect files and avoid discussing cases in public.

Safety note

Poor confidentiality can cause stigma and harm, including relationship conflict/violence.

3) Priority STIs / conditions for investigation

Priority conditions require faster investigation and stronger follow-up.

4) Contact tracing & follow-up workflow (high level)
  1. Receive referral / diagnosis notification
  2. Interview (IR) → identify contacts within tracing window
  3. Open FRs and document attempts/outcomes
  4. Ensure contacts are tested/treated (as per clinician direction)
  5. Close investigation with complete documentation and reporting
