Critical Period Calculator (Syphilis + Common STIs)

Based on the CI Field Guide “Maximum Infectious Period for Common STIs” table and the guidance to add the duration of symptoms to the maximum infectious period (Field Guide p.41). Use this tool for practice and learning.

Required Reading
CI Field Guide: p.41 (Critical period guidance + Table A).
How the calculator works Teaching
  • Critical Period (CP) is used to decide which contacts are in-scope for investigation and epi-treatment.
  • The Field Guide describes CP as: maximum infectious period + duration of signs and symptoms before treatment.
  • Example: if Secondary Syphilis has maximum infectious period 6 months, and symptoms were present for 2 weeks before treatment, your investigation window extends back by ~6 months + 2 weeks.
Result will appear here.
This tool supports learning and practice. Always follow your programme guidance and manuals.
Practice Reminder: CP helps guide (1) which contacts to elicit and follow up, and (2) when epi-treatment may be considered per programme rules.

DIA Workflow & Practice Script

A much more detailed DIA module is included inside the Modules section. This panel gives a quick reference flow you can practise aloud.

Simple DIA Flow (High-level)
  • Receive case alert → verify identifiers → check register / clinic records → confirm priority
  • Prepare locating plan → choose safest contact method → document attempts
  • Deliver message without unnecessary disclosure → arrange appointment/testing
  • Link to care and prevention services → update IR/FR and outcome fields
  • Daily/weekly reporting → quality check → DHIS2/MSSR submission flow
Required Reading
CI Field Guide: see p.40–41 for DHIS2/MSSR context and data flow (Field Guide p.40–41).
