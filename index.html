Practice: Interaction Script (DIA-style)

Purpose: Read this out loud to build confidence and reduce “script freeze”. Adjust wording to fit the client’s language level (simple, respectful, non-judgmental).
Example: Initial contact (phone / in-person)
  • Introduce & verify: “Good morning/afternoon. My name is ___, I’m calling from the health department. Am I speaking with ___?”
  • Privacy check: “Are you in a private place where you can talk for a minute?”
  • Purpose without details: “I’m calling about an important health matter. I’d like to meet you or speak with you briefly.”
  • Offer options: “We can talk now if you’re private, or we can set a time/place that works for you.”
  • Safety: “If at any point you feel uncomfortable, tell me and we can pause.”
Field Guide reference: Confidentiality section and interviewing principles; see p.8 and the interviewing/forms sections.
Example: Partner services / contact notification (simple language)
  • “Someone you were close with has an infection that can pass on during sex.”
  • “I can’t share who it is. My job is to help you get tested and treated if needed.”
  • “Even if you feel fine, you can still have it and pass it on.”
  • “We can help you today. If treatment is needed, it’s available.”
  • “Do you have any symptoms right now? (burning, discharge, sores, rash, pain)”
Reminder: Keep it factual, calm, and private. Never reveal the index case.
