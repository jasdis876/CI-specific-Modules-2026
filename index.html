Module 1 – Contact Investigation Fundamentals

Required Reading:
Participants MUST read the CI Field Guide pages covering:
  • Confidentiality – approx. pp. 8–9
  • Roles & Responsibilities – pp. 53–54
  • Critical Period concepts – pp. 41–42
The interface content below does not replace the Field Guide.

Contact Investigation (CI) is a structured public health process used to:

  • Interrupt transmission of HIV, STIs, TB, and other notifiable conditions
  • Identify exposed persons (contacts)
  • Link contacts to testing, treatment, and prevention services
  • Document outcomes for surveillance and programme monitoring

CI work is governed by strict confidentiality, ethical conduct, and legal reporting requirements under Jamaican public health law and MOHW policy.

Module 4 – HIV-Exposed Infants (HEI)

Required Reading: CI Field Guide HEI & paediatric sections (pp. 87–94)

HEI investigations are classified as priority cases. Delay in follow-up may result in severe morbidity or death.

  • Confirm maternal HIV status and treatment history
  • Ensure early infant diagnosis (EID)
  • Document infant prophylaxis and feeding method
  • Coordinate with paediatric HIV services

Module 5 – DIA & CI Workflow

DIA Framework
  • Detect / Define – referral, urgency, planning
  • Investigate – interview, timeline, contacts
  • Act – notify, link, document, close

DIA prevents partial CI work. A case is not complete until outcomes are verified and documented.
